### Second check with an audio decision model

In the `hybrid` eval mode, each flagged event gets a second check before scoring. Set it with `eval_mode="hybrid"` on the metric, or for every voice metric with [`DEEPEVAL_VOICE_EVAL_MODE`](/docs/environment-variables#deepeval-voice-eval-mode); the default, `deterministic`, calls no model and ignores `audio_decision_model`. Two models can do the check: `HumeDecisionModel()` (reads `HUME_API_KEY`), which is used when `audio_decision_model` is not set, or `OpenAIAudioDecisionModel()` with `gpt-audio` or `gpt-audio-mini` (reads `OPENAI_API_KEY`). A missing key fails when the metric is created. The model hears a clip of about five seconds around the event, from the test case's stereo `audio` when there is one and from the turn's own audio otherwise, and answers one yes/no question about it. An event is dismissed when the model judges it more likely not real than real. For Hume, that means fewer than half of its listeners would call it real; for OpenAI, a "no" it is more than 50% sure of, or a "yes" it is less than 50% sure of. A dismissed event no longer counts against the score and is left out of `events`; `dismissed_events` in the breakdown and one sentence in the reason say how many were dismissed. When no clip can be cut, or the model fails, the event stays and a warning is logged. Each check is one paid request, reported in `evaluation_cost`.
