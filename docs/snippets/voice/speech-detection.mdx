With `use_speech_detection` on (the default), the caller only counts as talking when the sound on their channel is both loud enough for that caller (at least 15% of their typical speech level) and heard as speech by the [Silero VAD](https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad) model, so background noise, bangs and hold music are not mistaken for the caller. Background voices, such as a TV, are still speech and still count. It needs `onnxruntime` (`pip install onnxruntime`), and the 2 MB model is downloaded once into `~/.cache/deepeval/models` (or `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/deepeval/models`). Set `use_speech_detection=False` to judge by loudness alone, where any sound on the caller's channel reads as the caller talking.
