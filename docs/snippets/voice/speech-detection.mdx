With `use_speech_detection` on (the default), the caller only counts as talking when the sound on their channel is both loud enough for that caller (at least 15% of their typical speech level) and heard as speech by the [Silero VAD](https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad) model, so background noise, bangs and hold music are not mistaken for the caller. Background voices, such as a TV, are still speech and still count. Without a recording, the same check finds where speech starts and stops inside each turn's own audio, for the agent's turns as well as the caller's, since a clip cut from a mixed recording can hold the other side's noise. There a short, faint word the model is sure of, such as a quiet "yes" or one cut in half where two clips meet, still counts. It needs `onnxruntime` (`pip install onnxruntime`), and the 2 MB model is downloaded once into `~/.cache/deepeval/models` (or `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/deepeval/models`). Set `use_speech_detection=False` to judge by loudness alone, where any sound on the caller's channel, or in a turn's audio, reads as that speaker talking.
