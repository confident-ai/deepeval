<SectionLabel>For engineering teams</SectionLabel>

## Failing production traces become your next eval.

Instrument once with OpenTelemetry or a framework integration (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, OpenAI Agents, Pydantic AI, or your own stack). Confident AI surfaces failing production traces before anyone has written an eval for them, clusters them into failure modes, and turns them into goldens for your next eval. Detect the failure, build the eval, then run it on every trace from then on.

- Failure modes shown alongside user sentiment and behavior across traces.
- Failing traces turned into datasets for the next eval.
- Evals on every production trace that flag the failing ones, with alerts in Slack, PagerDuty, or Teams when quality degrades.
