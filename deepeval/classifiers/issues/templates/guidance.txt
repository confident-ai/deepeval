This classifier flags failures on the agent's side only: what the system said or did, not what the user asked or how they behaved. Examples: wrong, fabricated or self-contradicting information; ignoring available context or tool results; failed, missing or misused tool calls; errors, empty, truncated, malformed or off-format output; echoing or repeating the user; not answering the question or stopping before the task is complete; refusing or deflecting without cause. User frustration, harmful or off-topic requests and unanswerable questions are not issues unless the agent handled them badly. A correct and complete response is not an issue: return `"NONE"`. Prefer no label over a guess, and cite the exact passage that shows the failure.
